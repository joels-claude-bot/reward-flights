| # | Round trip | Seats | Leave (12–24 Dec) | Return (31 Dec – 9 Jan) | Starts in |

:::warning Option (b) is the hard one — 3 seats over peak

- **Three seats together:** expect economy, split flights/dates or mixed cabins.
- **Three business seats on one peak flight:** very unlikely.
- **Options (a) and (c):** easier, with **one** seat each.

:::

:::tip Points don't care which order you fly — but taxes do

A return award combines two one-ways. Points work in either direction; taxes depend on the starting country.

- **Options (a) and (c): Australia** → no UK Air Passenger Duty (APD); typically **a few hundred pounds cheaper** per person.
- **Option (b): UK** → UK APD, especially costly in premium cabins; the 3-passenger trip pays it three times.

:::

### The others, briefly

- **Qantas Frequent Flyer (1:1, ~3 business days):** fallback for Perth or seats unavailable through Avios.
- **Qantas Classic:** 63,500 economy / 166,300 business one-way LHR–Australia; Christmas premium seats are rare.
- **Qantas Classic Plus:** more availability at roughly 2–3× the points.
  Also search Emirates and oneworld partners (Qatar, Malaysia via KL, Cathay).
  ([Point Hacks – Classic Plus](https://www.pointhacks.com.au/qantas/classic-plus-flight-rewards-guide/))
- **Singapore KrisFlyer:** low fees; ~108,500 miles business LHR–BNE.
  Amex UK ratio **3:2** means ≈162,750 MR points.
- **Use existing KrisFlyer miles only:** transfers take up to **~15 working days**; seats can disappear. ([Smart with Points](https://smartwithpoints.co.uk/p/breaking-amex-uk-removes-singapore-airlines-krisflyer-as-transfer-partner))
- **Live check (AwardTool):** no KrisFlyer space on LHR→PER at peak; a little appears on earlier Brisbane dates (e.g. 12 Dec), but the 3:2 ratio still makes it poor value. Skip.
- **Cathay / Asia Miles (1:1, ~3 days):** backup via HKG → BNE/PER.
  LHR–HKG–AUS costs ~179k business plus a fuel surcharge.
- **Emirates Skywards — avoid:** **2:1** Amex ratio plus **~£2,000 of return surcharges** in business. ([AFF](https://www.australianfrequentflyer.com.au/emirates-carrier-charges/))
- **Virgin Atlantic — skip:** 1:1 instant transfers, but no Australia flights or award partner serving it.

### Is 90,000 Avios good value? (rough EV)

You can't see exact taxes until you transfer Avios, so this is approximate. Value per Avios =
(cash fare you'd otherwise pay − the award's cash taxes) ÷ points.

| Leg (economy, 90,000 Avios) | Cash (best) | Est. taxes | Value / Avios |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| BNE→LHR (20 Dec) | £1,153 | ~£150 (ex-AUS) | ~1.1p |
| LHR→BNE (3 Jan) | £768 | ~£200 (ex-UK APD) | ~0.6p |
| PER→LHR (20 Dec) | £1,158 | ~£150 | ~1.1p |
| LHR→PER (Jan) | £946 | ~£200 | ~0.8p |

**Verdict: mediocre — roughly break-even.** Avios are worth about **1p each**; these economy legs
land around **0.6–1.1p** (≈0.9p round trip). So 90k for economy is **fair, not a bargain** —
Qatar's dynamic pricing (90k vs the old 45k) eats the value. Avios go much further in
**business/Qsuite (~1.5–2p+)** or **short-haul**. Worth it only if you'd otherwise pay the *higher*
cash fares, need these exact peak dates, or want to keep the cash. At the cheaper cash fares
(£650–£670) the value drops to ~0.4–0.6p — just pay cash. See the [calculator](/expected-value).

Taxes are estimates; the ±£100–200 unknown moves the value by only a few tenths of a penny.

- **Confirm the seat on the airline's own site before transferring.** Aggregators show *likely* space and can be stale or capped (AwardTool free tier: 8-day range, limited entries). The airline page is the only authority — this is the golden rule.
- **Exact taxes + UK APD** — I have the points, not the confirmed £ cash for a specific itinerary. Read it on the final booking screen.
- **Option c return (LHR→PER, January)** — no Qatar/Avios space. Decide between **Etihad** (not an Amex UK partner), **Qantas** (dynamic, pricey) or **cash (~£946)**.
- **Qatar's Virgin-Australia-operated frequencies (from 9 Dec)** — book the **QR-numbered** flight and confirm it opens as Avios, not the VA-numbered one.
- **Pin your exact date + programme** — I sampled a few days (12 / 19 / 20 Dec, 31 Dec, 3 Jan). Check your chosen day, and whether the ~62,500 on 12 Dec is Qatar Avios or Qantas Classic.
- **Then book:** find the seat → transfer MR → Avios (instant via BA Club) → book straight away.
